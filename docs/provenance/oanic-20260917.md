## Conflicts and exclusions

The old Teranova reply is not source evidence: it says six months while the supplier introduction says three months to pharmacy entry. Neither interval was used. Catalog p. 2 emphasizes maximum test outcomes; the partner proposal separates means and maxima. Moisture-retention figures also differ (catalog 89.26%, proposal maximum 80.82%); omitted. The 20-person reuse survey was not extrapolated to the hydration study. Older catalog roadmap and promotional pre-orders do not prove a commercial Oatmeal launch. Company superlatives, vegan implications for the salmon-PDRN Sahara product, registrations, certifications, full INCI, factory identity, clinical provider/protocol, export MOQ/Incoterms/lead time/exclusivity are not asserted.
