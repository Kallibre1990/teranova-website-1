About the company

DONGDONGGURIMOO CO., LTD. is a Korean cosmetics company based in Seoul, owner of the LEBELAGE and HEEYUL brands. LEBELAGE is a skincare and color cosmetics brand built around natural, functional formulas. HEEYUL is a specialized hair care brand, including an anti-hair-loss line.

The range covers the full care cycle: cleansers, toners, essences and ampoules, creams and serums, sheet masks and patches, sun care, body and hand care, plus color cosmetics and fragrance. The current price list includes 351 SKUs across 30 categories.

According to the company, products are supplied to overseas markets, including Russia and Southeast Asia. Some items carry registration numbers under MoCRA (US), CPNP (EU), and SCPN (UK).

Capabilities and range

Functional actives in the formulas

The lines are built around well-known cosmetic actives: collagen, centella (Cica), vitamin C, hyaluronic acid, PDRN, glutathione, retinol, peptides, panthenol, ceramides, niacinamide, snail mucin, and tea tree.

A broad product matrix

The largest categories in the price list are color cosmetics (47 items), sheet masks (42), creams (39), facial cleansers (32), and ampoules (25) — enough to build a starter assortment for a specific sales channel.

Registrations for export markets

The price list lists registration numbers: MoCRA (US) for 240 items, EU CPNP for 77, UK SCPN for 76. Current validity is confirmed by documentation at the deal stage.
